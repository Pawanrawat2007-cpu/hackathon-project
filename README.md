# 🚨 RAPID Rescue
### Smart Disaster Rescue & Coordination AI

> A smart disaster-response dashboard designed to help rescue teams monitor emergencies, coordinate resources, and respond faster during critical situations.

---

## 🌍 About the Project

*RAPID Rescue* is a smart disaster rescue and coordination platform created to support emergency response teams during natural and man-made disasters.

The dashboard provides a centralized view of active incidents, rescue teams, shelters, hospitals, alerts, and affected areas.

It helps responders quickly understand the situation and coordinate rescue operations efficiently.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

During disasters, emergency teams often face problems such as:

- Lack of real-time information
- Difficulty coordinating multiple rescue teams
- Delayed emergency response
- Poor communication between response units
- Difficulty tracking affected and rescued people
- Lack of centralized disaster information

RAPID Rescue aims to bring important emergency information together in one simple dashboard.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

RAPID Rescue provides a centralized emergency-response dashboard with:

- 🚨 One-tap emergency alert
- 🗺️ Live emergency monitoring map
- ⚠️ Real-time disaster alerts
- 👨‍🚒 Rescue team deployment tracking
- 🏥 Hospital and shelter information
- 👥 People rescued tracking
- 📊 Active incident statistics
- 📡 Live feed status
- 🔄 Auto-refreshing emergency updates

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🚨 One-Tap Emergency Alert
Allows users to quickly send an emergency alert with location and status information to the nearest rescue team.

### 🗺️ Live Monitoring Map
Displays important emergency zones and locations such as:

- Critical Zones
- Warning Zones
- Shelters
- Hospitals

### ⚠️ Real-Time Alerts
The dashboard displays emergency events such as:

- Emergency SOS alerts
- Earthquake detection
- Rescue team deployment
- Weather warnings
- Team arrival updates

### 👨‍🚒 Rescue Team Coordination
Helps track deployed rescue teams and their current emergency response activities.

### 🏠 Shelter & Hospital Support
Provides information about available shelters and hospitals during emergencies.

### 📊 Disaster Statistics
Displays useful information including:

- Active Incidents
- Teams Deployed
- People Rescued
- Shelters Open
